How the Composite Score Is Computed

1
Score each criterion 1–5

Every criterion (interstate distance, floodplain %, slope, etc.) is scored on a 1–5 scale against defined thresholds — 5 = best, 1 = worst. Distances where closer is better except hospital and school where farther is better; coverage & slope where less is better.

→
2
Weight criteria into domains

Within each domain, criteria are combined by a weighted average — more important criteria carry more weight — giving a 1–5 score per domain: Environment Transportation Energy Social

→
3
Weight the domains

Domain scores combine into one composite using these weights:

Energy
50%
Transportation
25%
Environment
20%
Social
5%
→
4
Convert to a percentage

The 1–5 composite is rescaled to an intuitive 0–100%:

Score % = (Composite − 1) ÷ 4 × 100

e.g. a composite of 4.5 → (4.5−1)/4×100 = 88%
